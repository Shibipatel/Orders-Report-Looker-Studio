# 📊 Orders Report Dashboard — Google Looker Studio

## 📁 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive *Orders & Fulfillment Analysis Report, built using **Google Looker Studio* (formerly Google Data Studio) and backed by *Microsoft Excel* sales data. 
The dashboard monitors operational order fulfillment across regional markets, evaluating customer segments, product categories, annual and seasonal monthly trends, shipping preferences, and geographical order distributions across 49 US states.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights
The dashboard includes the following key visualizations:

1. *Executive Scorecards & KPIs*
   - *Total Orders:* 9,994
   - *Unique Customers:* 793
   - *Covered States:* 49
   - *Customer Segments:* 3 (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)

2. *Order Volume Breakdown (Donut Charts)*
   - *# Of Orders by Region:* Distribution across West (32.2%), East, Central, and South.
   - *# Of Orders by Category:* Product distribution led by *Office Supplies (60.3%)*, followed by Furniture (21.2%) and Technology (18.5%).
   - *# Of Orders by Segment:* Customer order breakdown dominated by *Consumer (51.8%)*, Corporate (30.2%), and Home Office (18.0%).

3. *# Of Orders by City*
   - Bar chart ranking top municipal order hubs, led by *New York City, **Los Angeles, **San Francisco, **Seattle, and **Philadelphia*.

4. *Time-Series Fulfillment Trends*
   - *Orders Trend (Year):* Multi-year trajectory tracking growth and volume across 2014, 2015, 2016, and 2017 against an operational average benchmark line.
   - *Orders Trend (Month):* Monthly seasonality curve displaying order spikes and peak volumes during Q3 and Q4 (September, November, and December).

5. *Detailed Operational Roster Tables*
   - *Order Records Table:* Granular log tracking Order Date, Order ID, and Quantity.
   - *State & Regional Quantity Matrix:* Geographic ranking showing high-density delivery hubs led by *California (West - 7,667 units), **New York (East - 4,224        units), **Texas (Central - 3,724 units), and **Pennsylvania (East - 2,153 units)*.
   - *Top Customer Account Summary:* Customer ID vs. total purchased quantity (e.g., top accounts purchasing 130–150 units).

---

## 💡 Key Insights
The dashboard reveals the following key takeaways:

1. *Primary Demand Hub:* *California* and *New York* are the two largest state delivery markets, accounting for nearly 12,000 units delivered combined.
2. *Category Frequency:* *Office Supplies* accounts for over *60% of all orders placed*, serving as the high-velocity replenishment engine for the business.
3. *Consumer Segment Dominance:* Individual retail consumers account for *over half (51.8%)* of all transaction volume.
4. *Strong Q4 Seasonality:* The monthly trend curve highlights significant demand acceleration from August through December, peaking before year-end.
5. *Standard Shipping Preference:* The majority of shipments are routed via Standard Class, balancing transit speed and freight costs.

> *Filter Data:* Use the interactive left-hand control panel (Segment, Category, Ship Mode) to dynamically segment all KPIs, trend lines, and state delivery tables.
